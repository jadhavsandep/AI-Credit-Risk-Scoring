# AI-Credit-Risk-Scoring
**Credit Risk Scoring Project** is a Machine Learning project that predicts whether a customer is likely to repay a loan or default. It uses customer financial and demographic data to assess credit risk. The project includes data analysis, model building, evaluation, SQL queries, and Power BI dashboards to support smarter lending decisions. 
# 📊 Credit Risk Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes customer demographics, credit behavior, loan purposes, and risk patterns. It helps identify high-risk customers, understand borrowing trends, and support data-driven lending decisions.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 👥 Total Customers: 1000
- 🟢 Good Risk Customers: 700
- 🔴 Bad Risk Customers: 300
- 💰 Average Credit Amount: 3.27K
- 🏆 Maximum Credit Amount: 18.42K

### Dashboard Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics (Age, Gender, Housing)
- Identify Good Risk and Bad Risk customers
- Monitor credit risk distribution
- Analyze customer loan behavior
- Track average and maximum credit amounts
- Understand customer housing status
- Identify the most common loan purposes
- Analyze age distribution of borrowers
- Detect high-value loan customers
- Support data-driven lending decisions
- Improve credit risk management
- Reduce loan default risk
